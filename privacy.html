Wi-Fi Guard privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who uses it

Wi-Fi Guard is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account and devices.

What it accesses

Through Google's Smart Device Management API, it opens a live view of one Nest camera that the owner chooses. It does not read camera history, other devices, or any other Google data.

What it does with it

Video frames are checked on the owner's own computer to see whether a laptop is in its usual spot. Frames are not uploaded, stored, or shared. The only pictures kept are a few small example crops of that spot, which the owner saves on purpose, on their own computer.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Sign-in tokens are kept in the owner's computer's secure credential store.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page or the Nest app.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
