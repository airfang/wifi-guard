Wi-Fi Guard

Wi-Fi Guard is a small personal home tool. It runs on one home computer.

It looks at the owner's own Nest camera to check whether a laptop is sitting in its usual spot. When it is, the laptop is allowed on the home Wi-Fi. When it isn't, the laptop's Wi-Fi is paused.

It is used only by its owner, on their own Google account, camera and Wi-Fi. It is not offered to anyone else.
